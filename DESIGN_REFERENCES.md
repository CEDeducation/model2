# Design direction

OpenLab is designed as an original scientific workspace rather than a pixel-for-pixel copy of another product.

Principles:
- readable 15–16 px working text instead of micro-text;
- persistent research navigation;
- large document-like notebook editor;
- compact scientific inspectors where density is useful;
- subtle elevation, hover movement and motion without distracting from data;
- interactive sequence maps with direct manipulation;
- consistent entity linking across experiments, projects, inventory and workflows;
- keyboard-friendly global search.
